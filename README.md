# Index Time Tracker

A time tracker for back-of-book indexing work. Every project (book) has five clocks, one for each kind of work: **Flipping, Detailing (MR), Calibration, Final Assembly and Editing**. Click a clock to start it and click it again to stop it. The tracker adds everything up and makes reports and invoices.

It runs in your web browser, and **your time stays on your own computer.** Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

---

## Everyday use

The tracker has tabs at the top. It always opens on **Clocks**.

- **Clocks:** your hours and the clocks, for every day.
- **Reports:** reports, exports, invoices and the list of time entries.
- **Schedule** (only if you turn it on in *Extras*): when you plan to work each week.
- **Settings:** *Keep your time safe* and *Extras*. An orange dot on **Settings** means something that keeps your time safe is still off.

When a clock is running and you're on another tab, the **Clocks** tab shows today's hours in green.

- **Start and stop work:** click a clock. It fills with its color while it runs. Click it again to stop.
- **Reading a clock:** the hand is a minute hand. It starts at 12 and makes one full turn per hour. Each full hour colors in one twelfth of the face. After 12 hours the face keeps a light fill and starts coloring in again. The exact time is always written underneath.
- **More than one clock** can run at once, for example Detailing on one book while Flipping another. Your **worked hours** count that time once. Each book still gets its full clock time for **project time** (see *Reports*).
- **START BREAK** pauses every running clock. **END BREAK** starts the same clocks again.
- **Today / This week / This month** at the top show the hours you actually worked.
- **New project:** click **+ New** beside *Project* at the top of the clocks, type its name and press **Enter**.
- **Short names:** a book can have a short name (up to 18 characters) for the Clocks tab, like "SleepOUP". Set it when you create the book, or click the book's name to rename it. Reports, time entries, Excel, PDF, invoices and the Basecamp summary always use the full name. Hover over a short name to see the full one.
- **Embedding:** for the odd book that needs embedding, click the book's **⋯** and choose **Add Embedding clock**. A small **Embed** clock appears under that book's name; click it to start and stop, like any clock. While it runs, it fills up once a minute. It counts in worked hours, project time and invoices ("Embedding"). Reports show an Embedding column only for periods that have embedding time. **Remove Embedding clock** works only while the book has no embedding time, so none can be lost.
- **Up to 5 projects show at once.** With more, scroll the list of projects; the activity names stay at the top.
- **In the installed app, the window fits itself** to your projects when it opens and when you add, close or delete a project. The rest of the time it stays the size you give it. A maximised window is left alone.
- **Rename, close or delete a project:** click the **⋯** next to its name. You can also click the name itself to rename it.
- A click shorter than 5 seconds isn't saved, so an accidental double click doesn't leave a tiny entry behind.

## Reports

On the **Reports** tab, the Reports card has three tabs:

- **Worked hours:** your billable time, day by day, with what you worked on each day. Clocks running at the same time count once.
- **Project time:** the hours on each book, split by activity, for billing each book. Each book gets its full clock time, so this can add up to more than your worked hours. A note says so when that happens.
- **Closed projects:** finished books and the total time spent on each one.

Pick the dates with **Week / Month / Year / All / Custom…** and step through them with the **‹ ›** arrows. Weeks run **Monday to Sunday**.

**Export report** saves one file for the dates you picked:

- **Excel workbook:** three sheets, *Worked hours*, *Project time* and *Time entries*. The totals are real formulas, and the Time entries sheet has filter buttons, so it's ready for sorting or a pivot table.
- **PDF:** the same three parts, ready to print or send.

Days with no time say **"Did not work"**. For the current week, only the days up to today are listed.

## Time entries

The **Time entries** card lists every stretch of time for the dates you picked, grouped by day.

- **Fix or delete one:** point at the row and click the **pencil**. Change the times, the project, the activity or the note. **Delete** asks you to click a second time.
- **Forgot to start a clock?** Click **+ Add time** and type the start and end time.
- An end time earlier than the start time means the work went past midnight.

## Closing a finished project

When a book is done, click the **⋯** next to its name, then **Close project**, then click again to confirm. If you don't click again within 10 seconds, nothing happens. The tracker then:

- stops that project's clocks and takes it off the clock board;
- copies a summary to your clipboard (the total time, plus each activity you used) and reminds you to post your time on Basecamp;
- **keeps all its time.** It still counts in Worked hours and Project time, and it's listed on the **Closed projects** tab.

On the Closed projects tab, the **⋯** next to each project has **Copy summary** and **Reopen project**.

## Not forgetting to clock out

When a clock may have run by mistake, an orange bar appears at the top and the tab title blinks. Once **Pop-ups and sound** is on, you also hear a chime, and if the tracker is behind other windows, a Windows pop-up stays on screen until you click it. The chime and pop-up come back every 5 minutes until you answer.

- **Away from the computer** (Chrome and Edge, once *Away detection* is on): if you don't touch the mouse or keyboard anywhere on the computer for 5 minutes, the tracker notes when you left. When you come back, you choose:
  - **Keep the time**
  - **Take out the time away:** the clock carries on from when you came back.
  - **Stop them** at the moment you left.
- **Still working?** When a clock has run for 2 hours without a check, the tracker asks. You can change the 2 hours, or pick **Never**.
- **Tracker closed, or computer asleep or off** for more than 10 minutes with a clock running: choose **Keep the time**, or **Stop them** at the moment you left.

## Mini tracker (Chrome or Edge)

Click **Mini tracker** at the top right. A small strip opens and **stays on top of your other windows**, even while you work in another program. Park it in a corner of either monitor. It shows:

- **today's hours** and a **Start break** / **End break** button. The dot has the running activity's color; hover over the strip to see which clocks are running.
- **the tracker's questions:** the strip turns orange. *Still working?* has **Yes** and **No** (No stops the clocks when the question was asked). *Away* has **Keep** and **Take out**.

To start or stop a particular clock, use the main window. The mini tracker closes when the main tracker window closes, so keep the main window open. Minimised is fine.

## Using it as an app (Chrome or Edge)

Click the **Install** icon at the right end of the address bar. The tracker gets its own window, a taskbar icon and a Start-menu entry, and it opens even without internet. It uses the same saved time as the browser tab.

- **Taskbar badge:** the icon shows how many clocks are running, a dot while you're on a break, and a blinking badge when the tracker is asking you something.
- **Right-click the taskbar icon** for **Start break / End break** and **Stop all clocks**.
- **Pin it:** right-click the taskbar icon and choose **Pin to taskbar**.
- **Open it when Windows starts:** in the app window, click **⋮** (top right) → **App info** or **App settings**, and turn on **Start app when you sign in**.

## Keeping your time safe

- **In the browser:** every click is saved right away, so a power cut, a lost internet connection or a closed window loses nothing. A running clock keeps counting while the tracker is closed.
- **Auto-save file** (Chrome and Edge on a computer): a copy of everything, kept in a file you choose (for example in Documents or OneDrive) and updated after every change. Even if the browser's data gets cleared, your time is still in the file. On a new computer, click **Open existing** and pick the file.
- **Save backup / Restore:** a copy you can keep or move to another computer. In Firefox or Safari, which have no auto-save file, save a backup now and then.

The **Keep your time safe** card at the bottom of the page walks you through all of this. Once everything is on, it shrinks to one line: *Your time is protected*.

## Extras: notes, invoices and weekly schedule

These are **switched off unless you turn them on** in the **Extras** card at the bottom of the page. Most people only need the clocks and reports.

**Notes.** When you stop a clock, a small bar asks *What did you do?* Type something short, like "ch. 52-54" or "Pauly training", and press **Enter**, or click **Skip**. Notes you used before on that project are suggested as you type. Notes show up in Time entries, in the reports and on invoices.

**Invoices.** Pick a week, click **Export report**, then **Invoice**. The first time, you fill in your details: name, logo, who you bill, your rate, payment details, phone and email. After that, you see the invoice for the week, and **Save PDF** downloads it.

- **Date** is the Monday the week starts. **No. Invoice** is the following Monday as YYMMDD (the week of 7 Sep 2026 is invoice 260914).
- There's one line per day worked, with the hours from Worked hours.
- Each description is built from that day's projects, activities and notes, for example "Katzung's Pharmacology: Flipping Chapters 50-51, 58, 60". To change a description, edit the note on that time entry.
- Each day is rounded to 2 decimals, and the totals add up the rounded lines, so the maths on the page always checks out.

**Weekly schedule.** Adds a **Schedule** tab for telling Kevin when you plan to work. It's only information: it doesn't start, stop or change any clock.

- Drag across the half hours you plan to work. Start a drag on a filled slot to erase instead. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys, then Space.
- While you drag, a small label by the pointer shows the days and times, like "Thu 2:30-4pm". Each planned block shows its times at the top.
- **My time / Eastern (Kevin):** switches the grid between your time and Kevin's (Pennsylvania). The tab always opens in your time. The switch appears only when Kevin's time differs from yours. Daylight saving is handled automatically: EDT in summer, EST in winter.
- Step through weeks with **‹ ›**. **Show … to …** picks which hours the grid shows.
- The three boxes compare your plan with the time your clocks tracked: **Planned**, **Worked**, and how far ahead or behind you are.
- A thin dark line on the grid shows where your clocks actually ran, next to what you planned.
- **Same as last week** copies last week's plan. If this week already has a plan, it asks with a second click first.
- **Copy schedule** copies the week, **always in Kevin's time (Eastern)**, with a first line like "Eastern time (EDT)". Everything else in the tracker stays in your time. Under **What gets copied**, pick the format; the choice is remembered:
  - **Full:** one line per day, like "Monday → 9 AM to 1 PM, 2 PM to 5 PM", which pastes into two spreadsheet columns.
  - **Short:** planned days only, like "M:9am-1pm, 2-5pm", for a quick message.
- **Clear week** needs a second click.
- **Remind me when my planned time ends** (on unless you switch it off): if a clock is still running when a planned block ends, the tracker asks, with the same chime and pop-up as "Still working?". **Keep working** or **Stop clocks now**.
- **Show planned hours in reports** (on unless you switch it off): adds a Planned column to Worked hours, on screen and in the Excel and PDF files. Switch it off if you'd rather not show your plan to whoever gets the report.
- The Clocks tab shows your plan too: "2.67 h of 7 h planned" under Today and This week.
- The schedule is saved with your time, so it's in your auto-save file and backups too.

## Tips and troubleshooting

- **Use one browser on one computer.** Each browser keeps its own separate copy of your time. To move to another browser or computer, use the auto-save file (**Open existing**) or **Save backup / Restore**.
- **"Allow saving to …" appears:** click it. The browser wants your OK again before it updates the auto-save file. When Chrome asks, choose **Allow on every visit**, and you won't be asked again.
- **Clearing your browser's history or site data** also clears the tracker. Your auto-save file or backup brings everything back.
- **After an update, the tracker looks the same:** reload the page. If you installed it as an app, close the window and open it again.
- **An export doesn't download:** try again. If it keeps failing, reload the tracker.
- **No pop-up appears:** check that Windows isn't in *Do not disturb* (Settings → System → Notifications) and that notifications for Chrome or Edge are allowed there. **Test** in the Pop-ups and sound step shows one on demand.
- **No chime:** the browser only allows sound after you've clicked somewhere on the tracker since opening it. Any click, like starting a clock, is enough.
- **The tracker looks bigger on one monitor than the other:** that's Windows scaling, not the tracker. Go to **Settings → System → Display**, click each monitor at the top, and set **Scale** to the same value, or the closest the screens allow. Web apps can't override this.
- **On a phone** the tracker works as a web page, but the auto-save file and away detection only work in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

## Sharing it: putting it on GitHub Pages

This is for the person who shares the link. Everyone who opens the link gets their own, separate, private tracker.

1. On github.com, click **New repository**. Name it `index-time-tracker`, set it to **Public**, and create it.
2. Click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in these files and the **`lib`** folder, then click **Commit changes**:
   - `index.html`, `app.js`, `reports.js`, `style.css`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon.svg`
   - `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` are optional. They're instructions only.
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Branch*, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
4. After a minute or two, the tracker is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/index-time-tracker/`.

> **Don't upload** the `sample report` and `mockups` folders, `weekly-schedule.html` (the old standalone page; the tracker has it built in now), the two `.txt` notes, or any real invoice, like `invoice 260914P.pdf`. A public repository can be seen by anyone, and invoices have your phone number, email and payment details on them.

**Updating later:** upload the changed files again on GitHub, replacing the old ones. The site updates within a few minutes, and everyone's saved time stays where it is.

## For coworkers: getting started

1. Open the link in **Chrome or Edge**.
2. Click the **Install** icon at the right end of the address bar. The tracker gets its own window and a Start-menu shortcut, and it opens even without internet.
3. At the bottom of the page, the **Keep your time safe** card walks you through setup:
   - **Auto-save file → Create file.** Save it somewhere safe, such as Documents or OneDrive. When Chrome asks, choose **Allow on every visit**.
   - **Away detection → Turn on.** Allow both questions Chrome asks, for away detection and for notifications.
   - **Pop-ups and sound → Turn on.** You'll hear a test chime and see a test pop-up. Use **Sound off** if you'd rather have only the pop-up.
4. **Optional:** turn on notes, invoices or the weekly schedule in the **Extras** card.

**Is my time private?** Yes. The page is just a program that GitHub hands to your browser. Your time, and your invoice details if you use them, are saved only on your own computer: in your browser, and in your auto-save file. They're never sent to GitHub, to the person who shared the link, or anywhere else.
